What Is a Loot Goblin?

A loot goblin is a player who hoards every shiny thing the game offers — and, honestly, everything outside the game too. In video games, the loot goblin is the teammate who vacuums up every drop before anyone else has even seen it. At the tabletop, it's the player whose character must own every magic item in the dungeon — and whose real-world shelves sag under dice, miniatures, rulebooks and Kickstarter boxes.

The term is affectionate, not insulting. Every party needs a loot goblin: they read the item descriptions, they remember where the +1 dagger went, and their enthusiasm for the haul keeps the adventure moving forward.

Where did “loot goblin” come from?

Fantasy goblins have always been portrayed as hoarders of trinkets and treasure, and video games turned the joke into slang: treasure-hoarding goblin creatures show up across RPGs and looter games, and players started applying the name to themselves. MMO and looter-shooter communities spread it, and tabletop players adopted it instantly — because if anyone understands the urge to grab everything that glitters, it's a person who owns a bag of holding.

Signs you might be a loot goblin

  • Your character sheet's inventory page has an appendix.
  • You've kept a completely useless item “just in case” for eleven sessions.
  • Your unpainted-miniatures backlog is measured in years, not boxes.
  • You back dice and miniature Kickstarters the way dragons acquire hoards.
  • You're reading this on the site of a store literally called Loot Goblin Supply Co., considering merch about your own hoarding. Welcome home.

Loot goblin vs. dice goblin

A dice goblin specializes: polyhedral dice only, but in endless quantity. A loot goblin is a generalist — dice, minis, books, coins, maps, snacks. The two subspecies overlap heavily; most veteran players identify as both.

Is being a loot goblin a bad thing?

No — it's a badge of honor. The hobby runs on people who love its objects: the click of dice, the heft of a sourcebook, the smell of a freshly opened miniature. We named this store after loot goblins because we are loot goblins. Hoard responsibly.
