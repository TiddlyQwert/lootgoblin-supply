Dice Goblin Gifts for Her: A Field Guide to the Hoard

There's a woman in your life with a dice problem. Bags of them. A special box. Opinions about resin. She's a dice goblin, and she's genuinely easy to shop for once you learn the rules of the hoard. This guide is the shortcut. (Shopping for a boyfriend instead? There's a companion guide for that.)

Rule one: don't guess at dice

It feels obvious — she loves dice, buy dice. But goblins curate with terrifying specificity: this set rolls for her druid, that one is display-only, the metal ones come out for boss fights. Buying blind duplicates the hoard or misses her taste entirely. The fix is gifting around the dice: the gear, the humor, the ritual of game night. That's safe territory, and it proves you actually get the hobby.

What to avoid

Skip anything with official game branding from unofficial sellers — unlicensed merch is a rights problem and usually badly made. Skip generic "gamer" gifts that aren't about tabletop; the identity is the hoard, not gaming in general. And skip dice unless you've done reconnaissance: peek at the collection, note the colors she owns multiples of, and keep the receipt.
