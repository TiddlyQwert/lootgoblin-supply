Game Master Gift Ideas: What to Get the Person Who Runs Your Table

Your Game Master builds the world, voices forty-seven goblins, tracks everyone's hit points and pretends the party's nonsense was "anticipated all along." GM gifts should acknowledge the labor — and ideally fuel it. Here are ideas that actually land.

Gifts that say "we see the work"

  1. The "Game Master — I Make It Up As I Go" mug. Honest, accurate, and holds the coffee that powers session prep. Original hooded-GM art, here.
  2. The matching tee. Same confession, wearable. Game Master shirt.
  3. A genuinely good notebook. Lay-flat, dot-grid, pocket in the back for loose NPC names. GMs fill these faster than any other human category.
  4. A better GM screen. If theirs is held together by tape and hope, an upgrade lands hard — look for ones with customizable inserts.

Gifts that fuel the table

  1. Snacks that survive a four-hour session. Ungreasy, one-handed, silently-opened. This is a real skill. Ask the table.
  2. Index cards + a decent pen. Sounds boring; is not. Prep velocity doubles.
  3. Minis or tokens for recurring NPCs. The barkeep deserves a physical form by now.
  4. A "Roll for Initiative" shirt — the GM's two favorite words, in badge form. Here.

The free gift every GM actually wants

Show up on time, know what your character sheet says, and let the bit land. Then, if you also hand over a mug, you're the favorite player. We don't make the rules. (The GM does. Literally.)
