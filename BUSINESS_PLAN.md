# Loot Goblin Supply Co. — Business Plan (CANONICAL)

*Consolidated 2026-09-09 (S5b). Operational state changes go in JOURNAL.md.*

## Model
Print-on-demand merch, tabletop-RPG / dice-goblin culture niche. $0 startup capital.
- **Storefront (checkout):** Printify Pop-Up Store — https://loot-goblin-supply-co.printify.me
  (free, no card on file; Printify collects payment; profit → Printify Balance).
- **Brand hub / SEO funnel:** this repo → https://lootgoblin-supply.com (GitHub Pages).
- **Fulfillment:** Printify free plan; Monster Digital (tees), SPOKE (mugs). 2–7 business
  days production — stated honestly on site.

(assets: `/mnt/agents/upload/designs/` in interactive sessions; v2 = current).

## Compliance red lines
- Products/designs/tags: generic d20/dice/goblin themes ONLY. Never: "Dungeons &
  Dragons", "D&D", "Dungeon Master", WotC logos/creature names.
- Articles may reference game names editorially (nominative fair use).
- No fake reviews/metrics. Honor stated refund policy. Follow platform ToS.

## Traffic strategy (organic only, $0)
1. SEO content hub (8 indexable pages as of 9/9) → internal links to products.
2. Indexation: GSC verified (DNS, human); sitemap submitted; IndexNow key deployed.
3. Social accounts (Pinterest/Reddit) require human signup — request only when
   content base justifies it. No paid ads until ledger covers them.

## Money & rules
- Spending: $0 until revenue → then <$25 autonomous, ≥$25 human approval.
- Payouts accumulate in Printify Balance; revisit withdrawals >$20k.
- **Pivot rule:** zero sales after 3 weeks of genuine documented traffic effort
  (clock started 2026-09-07 → decision ~2026-09-28) → post-mortem + pivot.
  Runner-up niches: pickleball long-tails, nurse sub-niches, crochet/yarn.
- **Human-only steps:** KYC/payment signups, social account creation, GSC clicks.
- **Notifications:** commit messages with [STATUS]/[MILESTONE]/[URGENT]/[HUMAN NEEDED]
  prefixes → Discord via repo webhook.
- Cadence: launch phase (≤2 weeks) a few sessions/day OK; then ≤1 purposeful
  session/day. Every session ships something tangible or logs why idle.
