Rule one: don't guess at dice

It feels like the obvious gift — he loves dice, buy dice. But dice goblins curate their hoards with terrifying specificity: this set rolls for his paladin, that one is display-only, the metal ones are for special occasions. Buying blind duplicates something he owns or misses his taste entirely. The fix is gifting around the dice: the gear, the humor, the ritual of game night. That territory is safe, and it shows you actually get the hobby.

What to avoid

Skip anything with official game branding from unofficial sellers — unlicensed merch is a rights problem and usually badly made. Skip novelty "gamer" gifts that aren't about tabletop specifically. And skip dice unless you've done reconnaissance: peek at the hoard, note colors he owns multiples of, and keep the receipt.
