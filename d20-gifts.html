D20 Gifts: What to Actually Get the Dice Goblin in Your Life

The twenty-sided die is the universal symbol of the tabletop RPG table, which makes d20 gifts the safest category in the hobby: every player rolls one, every player has opinions about them, and nobody has ever said "no thanks, I already have too much d20 stuff." The trick is picking something that says you understand their table culture — not just the hobby in general.

Match the gift to the player

  • The dice goblin — owns seven sets, "needs" an eighth, names the pretty ones. Feed the hoard: the Dice Goblin tee or Dice Goblin mug is a self-aware gift that lands every time.
  • The lucky one — rolls suspiciously well and knows it. The Nat 20 Energy tee is basically a warning label. (Not sure what "nat 20" means? We explain it here.)
  • The chaos agent — their plans fail beautifully. Crit Happens (tee or mug) is the official slogan of rolling a 1 at the worst moment.
  • The game master — runs the world, improvises the rest. The Game Master — I Make It Up As I Go tee tells the truth they can't say aloud. More ideas in our GM gift guide.
  • The whole table — game-night mugs are the group gift that actually gets used weekly. We matched all eight of ours to table personalities in Best Mugs for Game Night.

What to avoid

Skip generic "gamer" gear that could be for video games — tabletop players are proud of the distinction. Skip novelty dice unless you know their taste (dice goblins are very particular). And skip anything with trademarked game logos unless it's officially licensed — unlicensed merch gets pulled, and gifted pulled merch is awkward for everyone. All of our designs are original, generic d20-and-goblin humor, so they're safe on that front.
